International book samples

These are browser-rendered reference texts for developing and checking the canvas editor's international text support. All three books are also available in the editor’s sample selector. The Chinese sample loads its own font set for horizontal layout.

  • Hayy ibn Yaqzan · حي بن يقظان
    Arabic · Ibn Tufayl. A continuous philosophical narrative with Arabic joining, vowel marks and long paragraphs.
  • Tashlikh · תשליך
    Hebrew · Isaac Erter. An 1840 satire with niqqud, German title matter, numbered notes and verse.
  • Journey to the West · 西遊記
    Chinese · Wu Cheng’en. All 100 chapters, traditional characters, dialogue and verse. Open in the canvas editor.

Each book includes its source, licence and conversion notes. Original text and combining marks are preserved; chapter headings have not been invented.
